Rates are interactions per kiloton-year, in observed energy after the detector response of step 5 (ES: electron kinetic energy; CC: visible energy E_ν − Q, no neutron or binding losses). Probabilities are interpolated from tables of the SNUfish solvers (Sun: B2 reference; Earth: NuFast-Earth), which they reproduce to 2×10⁻⁴ for rates and 1 % for the day-night asymmetry. Sampled (1σ): oscillation parameters, solar fluxes with their correlations, statistics. Model ranges are the spread of the available models, not probabilities, and are never folded into the σ.
